FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Guest sessions work without an account. Accounts sync settings and bookmarks.

What is deleted when a session ends?

Veyra deletes the session cookies, storage, browser context, VPN tunnel and session tabs.

Can I use Veyra on a phone?

Yes. The interface adapts to phone, tablet and desktop widths.
